2. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly necessary to provide commerce, rental management, worker dispatch, and customer support services:

  • Account & Contact Data: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Delivery Address, and PIN code.
  • Authentication Identity: Google Account identifiers (Google ID token, verified email, and profile name) provided via Google Sign-In and Android Credential Manager.
  • Commerce & Order History: Product items ordered, quantities, delivery site addresses, GST invoice numbers, UTR transaction references, and payment status.
  • Equipment Rentals: Rental requests, equipment types (demolition hammers, drills, concrete cutters), rental durations, and site deployment notes.
  • Technician & Worker Services: Service booking details, worker categories (Electricians, Plumbers, Painters, Masons), work scheduling, and completion status.
  • Technician Profiles (For Registered Workers): Trade skill, mobile number, daily labor rates, service area pincodes, and verification documents.

4. Data Storage, Security & Access Control

We implement zero-trust data protection standards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your device and Google Cloud servers utilize TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Confidential Business Data Protection: Internal purchasing costs, supplier details, warehouse cost valuations, and system audit logs are isolated with strict server-enforced security rules and are accessible exclusively to the authorized store administrator (mizaheerabad@gmail.com). Customers and technicians cannot query or read purchasing margins.
  • Customer Data Isolation: Customers can only access their own invoices, orders, rental requests, and profile details. Cross-account access is strictly blocked by Cloud Firestore security rules.
  • Financial Safety: We do not store sensitive payment card details or banking passwords. Payments are confirmed via verified UTR / UPI transaction references or cash on site delivery.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Mandate)

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies, users have complete control over their personal data and account:

How to Delete Your Account and Data

You have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated personal records at any time:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open the App → Navigate to Account tab → Scroll to Account Management → Tap Delete Account & Data. After confirmation, your profile record, address, contact numbers, and authentication credentials are automatically purged from Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication.
  2. Email / Web Deletion Request: If you are unable to access the app, you may email our data protection officer at mizaheerabad@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" using your registered email address. We will verify your identity and permanently delete your account within 7 business days.

Note: Transactional tax invoice records may be archived for the minimum period required by Indian GST / commercial law for accounting compliance, after which they are destroyed.
